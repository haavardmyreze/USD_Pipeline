# Future ideas

Ideas not worked through yet. One that is taken up becomes a spec, or changes one, and leaves this list.

## Content root above the project

Lay the [[Project tree]] out like Unreal Engine: a content root, with the project folder inside it. Libraries and assets from earlier projects could then be migrated between projects without their paths changing.

## Shared node graphs

How shared USD `NodeGraph` prims are handled. These are shading networks that many materials reuse, like material functions in Unreal Engine. The idea covers where they live, how materials bring them in, and how they are named.

## Global variables

How global variables are structured and where they are defined, beyond the per-workfile ones in [[Environment variables]].
